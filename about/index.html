I'm Ioannis Kapsalis, a Junior System Engineer at Netcompany, based in Athens. My work sits between system administration and DevOps — what I call DevOps on bare-metal: building Linux servers, automating how they are configured, and making them observable.

I learn by building. Each lab on this site starts as a few VMs or containers on my machine and ends as a written guide I can repeat later — the commands, the configuration, and the mistakes I hit along the way.
